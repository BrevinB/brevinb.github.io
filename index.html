About the work

I build apps in my free time, for fun — every one of them has a story behind why it exists. Having thousands of people actually using them is what keeps me motivated to make each one better every day.

Every app above is a solo build: idea, design, Swift code, App Store listing, and post-launch updates. Six of the eight shipped in the last twelve months.

The lineup covers real product range — health & fitness tracking, an AI-powered study tool, a TCG collection scanner, an online multiplayer game, and habit-building utilities.
