Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started

Q: How do I add cards to my collection?
A: There are three ways to add cards:

  1. Scan with Camera: Tap the "Scan" tab and point your camera at a physical card
  2. Manual Add: Tap "Manual Add" in the Scanner tab to browse and select cards
  3. Bulk Import: Import your existing collection from Dreamborn or CSV files in Settings

Q: Do I need an internet connection?
A: The app works completely offline for viewing your collection and building decks. An internet connection is only needed for:

  • Fetching current card prices
  • Downloading new set data
  • Importing data from online sources

Q: Is my data stored in the cloud?
A: No. All your collection data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData. Your data is never uploaded to our servers. If you have iCloud Backup enabled, your collection will be included in your device backups.

Collection Management

Q: How do I track different card variants?
A: When adding a card, you can select the variant (Normal, Foil, Borderless, Promo, Enchanted). Each variant is tracked separately in your collection.

Q: Why isn't my collection showing up-to-date prices?
A: Prices are fetched in the background when you add cards. To refresh all prices manually, go to Settings and tap "Refresh All Prices." Note: This may take a few minutes for large collections.

Q: Can I track how many copies of each card I own?
A: Yes! Each card in your collection shows a quantity counter. You can adjust quantities in the card detail view.

Q: How do I remove a card from my collection?
A: Tap on any card in your collection, then tap the "Remove from Collection" button at the bottom of the detail screen.

Deck Building

Q: What deck formats are supported?
A: Inkwell Keeper supports:

  • Core Constructed: 60 cards, max 4 copies per card
  • Infinity Constructed: 60 cards, unlimited copies allowed

Q: How do I know if my deck is legal?
A: Open any deck and tap "Validate Deck." The app will check:

  • Total card count (60 cards required)
  • Maximum copies per card (format-specific)
  • Ink color restrictions

Q: Can I see which cards I'm missing for a deck?
A: Yes! Each deck shows a "Missing Cards" section that lists cards you need to complete the deck, along with their current prices.

Q: How do I export my deck list?
A: Open a deck and tap the share button to export your deck list as text. You can copy, share, or save it.

Scanning

Q: The camera scanner isn't recognizing my cards. What should I do?
A: Try these tips:

  • Ensure good lighting (avoid glare and shadows)
  • Hold the card steady and fill most of the frame
  • Make sure the card art is clearly visible
  • If scanning fails, use "Manual Add" to browse and select the card

Q: Can I scan multiple cards at once?
A: Currently, the scanner processes one card at a time. For adding multiple cards quickly, consider using the Bulk Import feature with a CSV file.

Data Management

Q: How do I backup my collection?
A: Your collection is automatically included in your device's iCloud backup (if enabled). You can also export your collection data:

  • Go to Settings > Export Collection
  • Save the CSV file to Files, iCloud Drive, or another location
  • Import this file later to restore your collection

Q: How do I import my collection from Dreamborn?
A:

  1. Export your collection from Dreamborn as a CSV file
  2. Open Inkwell Keeper and go to Settings > Import
  3. Select "Import from Dreamborn"
  4. Choose your CSV file
  5. Review and confirm the import

Q: Can I transfer my collection to a new device?
A: Yes! Use one of these methods:

  1. iCloud Backup: Restore your new device from an iCloud backup
  2. Export/Import: Export your collection as CSV from your old device, then import it on your new device

Q: How do I delete all my data?
A: Go to Settings > Delete All Data. This will permanently remove your entire collection, decks, and wishlist. This action cannot be undone.

Pricing

Q: Where do card prices come from?
A: Prices are sourced from public TCG marketplaces and represent average market values. Prices are estimates and may not reflect exact retail or secondary market values.

Q: How often are prices updated?
A: Prices are fetched when you add new cards. To manually refresh all prices, go to Settings > Refresh All Prices.

Q: Why do some cards show $0.00?
A: This can happen if:

  • The card is very new and pricing data isn't available yet
  • Pricing data failed to fetch (try refreshing)
  • The card is from a promotional set with limited market data

Sets and Completion

Q: Which Disney Lorcana sets are included?
A: Inkwell Keeper includes all released Disney Lorcana sets:

  • The First Chapter
  • Rise of the Floodborn
  • Into the Inklands
  • Ursula's Return
  • Shimmering Skies
  • Azurite Sea
  • Reign of Jafar
  • Whispers in the Well
  • And all future releases!

Q: How is set completion calculated?
A: Set completion is based on unique cards you own from that set. Each variant counts separately. The percentage shows how many different cards you've collected out of the total set size.

Statistics

Q: What statistics can I view?
A: The Stats tab shows:

  • Total collection value
  • Total cards owned (including quantities)
  • Unique cards collected
  • Rarity breakdown (Common, Uncommon, Rare, etc.)
  • Set completion percentages
  • Recent additions to your collection

iPad Support

Q: Does Inkwell Keeper work on iPad?
A: Yes! Inkwell Keeper is optimized for iPad with:

  • Larger grid layouts (4-5 columns)
  • Sidebar navigation (iOS 18+)
  • Split-screen support
  • All features available on iPad

Wishlist

Q: How do I add cards to my wishlist?
A: Browse any card and tap "Add to Wishlist" in the detail view. Your wishlist appears in the dedicated Wishlist tab.

Q: Can I move wishlist cards to my collection?
A: Yes! Tap any card in your wishlist and select "Move to Collection." This removes it from your wishlist and adds it to your collection.
