Common questions

My doubloon purchase didn't arrive

Force-quit the game and reopen it — purchases are re-checked at launch. If the doubloons still haven't arrived, email us with your App Store receipt and we'll make it right.

How do I start my progress over?

Open Settings → Reset Profile inside the game. This wipes your local profile (doubloons, unlocks, and captain progress) and cannot be undone.

How do online matches work?

Online battles use Apple's Game Center. Make sure you're signed in to Game Center in your device Settings, then head to the Harbor to challenge a friend or find an opponent.

How do I turn analytics off?

Open Settings → Anonymous Analytics and flip the toggle. Details on what little we collect are in our Privacy Policy.
