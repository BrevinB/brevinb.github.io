Kyber Vault: Card Scanner

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

The short version

Kyber Vault doesn't collect personal information, show ads, or track you. There are no accounts. Anonymous usage statistics help improve the app, and you can turn them off in Settings.

Your collection

Your cards, wishlist, decks, and binders are stored on your device and synced through your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. Only you can see them; the developer can't.

Card data and images

Card details and images are downloaded from swu-db.com. Like any website, it can see your device's IP address when it sends them. Images you view are saved on your device so they work offline.

Prices

Daily TCGplayer prices come from Kyber Vault's own price server. The app asks it for the day's prices and the history of the cards you look at or own; nothing identifies you, and the server keeps no record of who asked. Like any server, it sees your device's IP address.

Usage statistics

Kyber Vault uses TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service, to count how features are used, for example that a batch of cards was scanned or a deck was created. Signals contain no names, emails, card lists, or advertising identifiers, and the anonymous device identifier is hashed on your device before it is sent, so it can't be traced back to you. Nothing is used for advertising or shared with data brokers. Turn this off any time in Settings › Privacy › Share Usage Statistics.

Purchases

Kyber Vault Pro is purchased through Apple. Subscription status is managed by RevenueCat using an anonymous identifier; neither RevenueCat nor the developer sees your name or payment details.

Camera

The scanner uses the camera to read the set and number printed on your cards. Images are processed on your device and never uploaded or stored.

Children

Kyber Vault is made for a general audience and doesn't knowingly collect information from children.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete All Collection Data removes everything from this device and iCloud. Deleting the app removes its local data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and in the app with a new date.
