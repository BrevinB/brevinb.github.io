Overview

Inkwell Keeper (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our Disney Lorcana collection tracking application.

Information Collection and Use

Data Storage

Inkwell Keeper is a local-first application. All of your collection data, decks, wishlists, and preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple’s SwiftData framework. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal information or collection data on external servers.

Types of Data Stored Locally

The app stores the following information on your device:

  • Card collection data (cards you own, quantities, variants)
  • Wishlist information
  • Deck builds and deck statistics
  • Set completion progress
  • App preferences and settings

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, or contact information
  • Your Apple ID or device identifiers
  • Your location data
  • Any personally identifiable information

Third-Party Services

Pricing Data

The app fetches current market pricing information for Disney Lorcana cards from public third-party sources. These requests are made anonymously and do not include any information that identifies you or your device.

Card Images and Data

Card information and images are sourced from publicly available Disney Lorcana databases and APIs. No personal information is transmitted when accessing this data.

Affiliate Links

The app may include affiliate links to authorized card retailers. When you click these links, you will be directed to external websites. We do not track your purchases or receive any personal information from these transactions. The retailer’s privacy policy will apply once you leave the app.

Camera Access

The app requests access to your device’s camera for the card scanning feature. Camera access is used solely to:

  • Scan physical Disney Lorcana cards to add them to your collection
  • Process card images locally on your device

We do not store, transmit, or access your photos or camera roll. The camera is used only during active scanning sessions within the app.

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your data is protected by your device’s security features (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID)
  • Your data is included in your device’s iCloud backup (if enabled)
  • We recommend keeping your device secure and up to date

Children’s Privacy

Inkwell Keeper does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app is designed for general audiences and is rated 4+ on the App Store. Since the app stores all data locally and does not collect personal information, it is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to Your Data

You have full control over your data:

  • You can delete individual cards, decks, or wishlist items at any time
  • You can export your collection data from the app
  • You can delete all app data through the Settings screen
  • Uninstalling the app will remove all data from your device

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy within the app
  • Updating the “Last Updated” date at the top of this policy

Third-Party Links

The app may contain links to third-party websites (card retailers, pricing sources). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before providing any information.

Data Retention

Your collection data remains on your device until you choose to:

  • Delete individual items
  • Clear all data through the app settings
  • Uninstall the application

Analytics

Inkwell Keeper does not use any analytics services. We do not track your usage, behavior, or any other information within the app.

Consent
