Description

One sentence: Kyber Vault is a free iPhone and iPad app that scans Star Wars: Unlimited cards in batches, tracks what a collection is worth, and knows which binder or box every card is in.

Short: Kyber Vault turns a pile of Star Wars: Unlimited cards into an organized, valued collection. Hold cards up to the camera one after another and they're read and batched as you go, and you can mark a card Foil or Hyperspace while it's still in frame. Daily TCGplayer prices show a collection's value, set and master-set progress tracks every variant, and the Cargo Hold mirrors real binders, crates and deck boxes, so finding a card takes seconds. It also builds Premier and Twin Suns decks and keeps score for 1v1 games and 3–4 player pods. Built by a solo developer, free with no ads, and collections stay on the user's device.

Story angles

The scanner that lets you pick the variant mid-scan

Star Wars: Unlimited prints most cards in several versions, and a Hyperspace Foil can be worth many times its standard copy. Most scanners log the card and leave the variant for later. Kyber Vault lets collectors switch the printing while the card is still in frame, then review the whole stack and add it at once, so a booster box takes minutes.

A companion for new players opening Icons

Icons, the game's new entry-level mini-set, brings a wave of first-time collectors. Kyber Vault gives them a free way to log their first packs, see what they pulled, and start a binder, with no account to create.

Your real shelves, in the app

The Cargo Hold models a collector's physical storage: binders with real pages, crates, deck boxes. Ask where a card is and it answers with the binder, page and pocket.

Game night for a whole pod

Twin Suns, the game's multiplayer format, gets a scorekeeper built for one phone in the middle of the table: base damage for every player, initiative, the Force, a round timer and a record for every deck.

Key features

  • Batch scanning: scan card after card, pick Foil, Hyperspace or Showcase in frame, review and add once
  • Prices and value: daily TCGplayer market prices, total collection value, most valuable cards
  • Set and master-set progress: completion across every set and variant, with missing-card filters
  • Cargo Hold: binders with page turns, crates, deck boxes and cases; find which page or box a card is in
  • Decks: Premier and Twin Suns builder with aspect penalties, swudb and Karabast import and export, and Test Draw
  • Holotable: a scorekeeper for 1v1 and 3–4 player Twin Suns, with share-ready Victory images
  • Import: TCGplayer exports, swudb collections and spreadsheets
  • Private by design: no account, works offline, iCloud sync
