ExtraMile+ ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

ExtraMile+ collects the following information to provide and improve the app:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication credentials via Firebase Authentication.
  • Run Data: Mileage, dates, and optional notes you enter are stored in Google Firebase Firestore to sync your data across devices.
  • Analytics: We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data such as app opens, feature usage, and crash reports. This data does not personally identify you.
  • Purchase Information: Subscription status is managed by RevenueCat. We do not store your payment details; all transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve ExtraMile+ features
  • To sync your run data across your devices
  • To manage your subscription status
  • To analyze anonymous usage trends and fix bugs

Third-Party Services

ExtraMile+ uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics)
  • RevenueCat (Subscription management)

Data Retention

Your run data is retained as long as your account is active. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app or by contacting us.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and Firebase security rules, to protect your data.

Children's Privacy

ExtraMile+ is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
